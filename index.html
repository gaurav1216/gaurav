Our Story

A boutique built on craft.

Mithila Boutique began in 2018 as a small atelier in old Jaipur, with a single rule: stock only what we'd wear and love ourselves.

Today we work with over forty independent karigars across India — from the weavers of Varanasi and Kanchipuram to the block-printers of Bagru and the kashida artisans of Srinagar. Every saree, every dupatta, every craft passes through our hands before it reaches yours.

  • Considered. Limited editions, never mass-produced.
  • Crafted. Handloom textiles and traditional techniques.
  • Personal. Styling appointments and bridal consultations.
