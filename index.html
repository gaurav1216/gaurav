Our Story

A boutique built on slow.

Maison Élise opened in 2018 as a small atelier on a quiet street, with a single rule: stock only what we'd wear, use, and love ourselves.

Today we work with thirty independent designers across Europe, Japan, and the Americas — most of whom we've visited in person. Every garment, every object passes through our hands before it reaches yours.

  • Considered. Limited drops, never overstocked.
  • Crafted. Natural fibres and traditional methods.
  • Personal. Styling appointments by request.
