Our Story

From Sita's land,
by her granddaughters.

Mithila Boutique began in 2019 as a small studio in Madhubani town — born from a simple wish: to take the painting tradition that adorns every village wall in our region, and put it on cloth that the world could wear.

Today we work with over sixty women artisans across the Mithila region — Madhubani painters from Ranti and Jitwarpur, Sikki grass weavers from Raiyam, Sujani embroiderers from Bhusura, and Tussar weavers from Bhagalpur. Every piece carries the name of the woman who made it.

  • Rooted. Crafts from the Mithila region only.
  • Hand-made. Natural pigments, handloom silks, no shortcuts.
  • Fair. Artisans set their own prices; we add a fixed margin.
